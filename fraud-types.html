Types of Online Fraud

Understand common cyber frauds, learn how scammers operate, and discover how you can protect yourself.

💳 1. UPI & Payment Fraud

How it happens: Scammers trick people into approving unknown UPI requests or sharing payment information.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected collect requests
  • Unknown QR codes
  • Pressure to pay immediately

Stay safe: Check the receiver's name and amount. You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

🔐 2. OTP & PIN Scams

How it happens: Fraudsters pretend to be bank employees or trusted service providers and ask for confidential codes.

Warning signs:

  • Requests for OTP or UPI PIN
  • Threats of account closure
  • Unexpected verification calls

Stay safe: Never share your OTP, PIN, password, or CVV with anyone.

🎣 3. Phishing & Fake Links

How it happens: Fake messages and websites imitate banks, delivery services, or official portals to steal information.

Warning signs:

  • Suspicious links
  • Spelling mistakes in web addresses
  • Messages demanding urgent action

Stay safe: Verify the website address and use official apps or websites instead of unknown links.

💼 4. Fake Job Scams

How it happens: Fraudsters offer fake jobs and demand registration fees, deposits, or payments for training.

Warning signs:

  • Guaranteed high salary for easy work
  • Payment demanded before joining
  • No verifiable company details

Stay safe: Verify the company through its official website and never rush into paying fees.

📱 5. Social Media Scams

How it happens: Fake profiles, impersonation, malicious links, and fake giveaways are used to deceive users.

Warning signs:

  • Requests for money from unusual accounts
  • Fake prize notifications
  • Unknown login links

Stay safe: Verify identities independently, use strong passwords, and enable two-factor authentication.

🏦 6. Banking Fraud

How it happens: Criminals use impersonation, stolen credentials, or deceptive calls and messages to gain access to accounts.

Warning signs:

  • Unrecognised transactions
  • Unexpected account alerts
  • Calls asking for confidential banking details

Stay safe: Contact your bank using its official number and enable transaction alerts.

⚠️ Remember: Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, or password. If you suspect financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly and report it through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal .
