Important Safety Information

Remember These Important Points

  • Never share your OTP, PIN, password or banking details with anyone.
  • Do not click suspicious links or install unknown applications.
  • Contact your bank immediately if you notice an unauthorized transaction.
  • For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 as soon as possible.
  • Keep screenshots, transaction IDs and other evidence related to the fraud.
  • Use official government websites to report cybercrime.
