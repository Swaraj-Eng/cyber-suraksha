Report Cybercrime

If you experience online fraud or notice suspicious digital activity, report it through official channels. Acting quickly can help protect your money, accounts and personal information.

🚨 Lost Money in Online Financial Fraud?

Contact the national cybercrime helpline as soon as possible.

1930

Keep your transaction details and complaint information ready.

🌐

1. Official Cybercrime Portal

Use the Government of India's official portal to report cybercrime and follow the instructions provided there.

Visit Official Portal ↗
🏦

2. Contact Your Bank

If money was transferred without your permission or you suspect financial fraud, contact your bank or payment provider immediately.

  • Use the official bank helpline.
  • Ask about blocking or securing affected services.
  • Keep the complaint reference number.
📄

3. Keep Evidence Ready

Collect useful information before submitting your complaint.

  • Transaction ID or UTR number
  • Date, time and amount involved
  • Suspicious phone numbers or website links
  • Relevant messages and screenshots
🔐

4. Secure Your Accounts

Take steps to prevent further unauthorized access.

  • Change compromised passwords.
  • Contact your bank if financial details are exposed.
  • Sign out of unfamiliar sessions.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
📝

5. Submit Accurate Details

Explain what happened clearly. Provide accurate dates, amounts, contact details and evidence requested by the official portal.

⚠️

6. Beware of Recovery Scams

Fraudsters may promise to recover your money in exchange for a fee. Do not share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs with anyone claiming they can recover your funds.

Important Safety Note: Report genuine incidents through official channels. Do not make false complaints, and never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN or full banking credentials in a complaint message or with an unknown person.
