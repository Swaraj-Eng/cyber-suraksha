Stay Safe Online

Simple digital safety habits can protect your money, personal information, and online identity.

🔐 1. Password Security

Why it matters: Weak or reused passwords can make your accounts easier to compromise.

  • Use long, unique passwords for different accounts.
  • Never share your password or OTP.
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever available.
  • Use a trusted password manager if needed.

💳 2. UPI & Payment Safety

Why it matters: Fake payment requests can trick you into sending money.

  • Check the receiver's name and amount before paying.
  • Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Do not approve unexpected collect requests.
  • Never share your card PIN, CVV, or OTP.

🎣 3. Fake Links & Messages

Why it matters: Fraudulent websites may steal login details or payment information.

  • Avoid clicking suspicious links in SMS or emails.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Download apps from official app stores.
  • Verify urgent messages using official contact details.

📱 4. Mobile & App Safety

Why it matters: Unsafe apps and excessive permissions can put your data at risk.

  • Keep your phone and apps updated.
  • Install apps only from trusted sources.
  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Never install unknown APK files sent by strangers.

🌐 5. Social Media Privacy

Why it matters: Oversharing personal information can help scammers impersonate you.

  • Keep your privacy settings updated.
  • Do not accept every unknown friend request.
  • Never share passwords or sensitive documents publicly.
  • Verify requests for money, even from familiar profiles.

📶 6. Wi-Fi & Public Devices

Why it matters: Untrusted networks and shared devices can expose your information.

  • Avoid banking transactions on untrusted public Wi-Fi.
  • Prefer your mobile network for sensitive transactions.
  • Log out after using a shared computer.
  • Never save passwords on public devices.
⚠️ If online financial fraud occurs: Contact your bank immediately, call India's cyber financial fraud helpline 1930, and submit a complaint at the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal . Keep transaction IDs, messages, and other evidence.
